About

A low-voltage contractor that talks like your estimator.

Sunstone Data Systems is a low-voltage contractor serving Dallas–Fort Worth and Texas. We take on the full communications and security scope for commercial and multifamily projects and manage it from takeoff to closeout.

How we're set up

Subcontractor to the GC. Accountable for the whole scope.

We contract with the general contractor or developer for the low-voltage package, then staff the job with trained installation crews under our supervision. One point of contact for the estimate, the schedule, the installation, and the closeout documents.

Sunstone Data Systems is a wholly owned subsidiary of Southroad Holdings Inc., headquartered in Dallas, Texas.

We work best on large, multi-building projects where the low-voltage scope is broad: fiber backbone, unit cabling, network rooms, cameras, and access control all on the same site.
